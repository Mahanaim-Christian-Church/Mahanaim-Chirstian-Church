Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

Mahanaim Christian Church ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you submit a form (e.g., event registration, prayer requests), we collect your name, email, phone number, and any message you provide.
  • Analytics Data: We use privacy-focused analytics (Umami) to understand how visitors use our site. This includes page views, section engagement, and interaction events. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected through analytics.
  • Technical Data: Standard web server logs may record your IP address, browser type, and referring URL. This data is used for security and performance monitoring only.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and registration requests
  • Improve our website experience and content
  • Understand visitor behavior through aggregated analytics
  • Communicate about church events, services, and ministry opportunities (only if you opt in)

Analytics and Tracking

We use Umami Analytics, a privacy-focused, open-source analytics platform. Umami does not use cookies, does not track users across websites, and does not collect personally identifiable information. All data is anonymized and aggregated.

We track custom events such as button clicks, form submissions, and section views to improve user experience. These events do not contain personal data.

Third-Party Services

Our website integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Formspree: For form submissions. Data is transmitted securely to Formspree and then forwarded to our email. Review Formspree's privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • YouTube: Embedded videos are served by YouTube. YouTube may set cookies. Review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet: Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap contributors. No personal data is shared.
  • Bible API: Scripture content is fetched from a public Bible API. No personal data is transmitted.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of communications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@mahanaim.org.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
