Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Mahanaim Christian Church respects your privacy. This static website is designed to share church information, locations, events, sermons, and ministry details without collecting unnecessary personal information.

Analytics

We may use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool, only when a valid website ID is configured. Umami is cookieless and is used for aggregate website improvement.

Tracked events may include nav clicks, CTA clicks, section views, video clicks, map marker opens, directions clicks, ministry card clicks, and outbound links.

We do not use analytics to track names, email addresses, phone numbers, prayer requests, form text, or sensitive personal data.

Third-Party Services

Maps are displayed with Leaflet and OpenStreetMap tiles. Sermon links may open YouTube. These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@mahanchurch.org.
