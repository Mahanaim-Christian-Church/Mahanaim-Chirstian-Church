Our Story

About Mahanaim
Christian Church

Mahanaim Christian Church was founded on a simple yet profound conviction: that the presence of God transforms every life it touches. Named after the place where Jacob encountered the angels of God (Genesis 32:2), our church stands as a place where heaven meets earth — a camp of God's people gathered in worship, prayer, and the study of His Word.

We are a Spirit-filled, Bible-believing community that warmly welcomes everyone — regardless of background, season of life, or spiritual journey. Whether you are seeking God for the first time or have walked with Him for decades, there is a place for you among us.

Our ministry is rooted in four pillars: Worship that honours God in spirit and in truth; Prayer that moves heaven and transforms hearts; The Word taught faithfully and applied practically; and Fellowship that builds authentic community in Christ.

"Jacob called the name of that place Mahanaim, saying, 'This is God's camp.'"
— Genesis 32:2 NKJV
